FastGallery Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version: FastGallery collects nothing. The app has no internet access, no ads, no analytics and no accounts. Your photos and videos stay on your device.

What FastGallery is

FastGallery is a photo and video gallery app for Android. It shows, organizes and edits the media files that are already stored on your device.

Information the app collects

None. FastGallery does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third party tracking code.
  • There are no accounts, no sign in and no cloud sync.
  • Your photos, videos, albums, favorites and settings are stored only on your device.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Photos and videos, storage, and all files access: to find, show, move, rename, hide and delete your media files, including files in hidden folders.
  • Media location: to show where a photo was taken, when the photo itself contains that information. The location is read from the file and shown to you only.
  • Notifications: to show the progress of long file operations.
  • Set wallpaper: to set a photo as your wallpaper when you ask for it.
  • Fingerprint or biometrics: to unlock hidden or protected folders, when you turn that feature on. Your biometric data is handled by Android and is never available to the app.
  • Run at startup and keep awake: to keep the media list ready so the app opens quickly, and to finish file operations and slideshows.

Everything these permissions allow happens on your device.

Sharing

FastGallery shares nothing by itself. When you choose Share on a photo or video, Android hands that file to the app you pick. What that app does with the file is covered by its own privacy policy.

Purchases

If you buy FastGallery on Google Play, the payment is handled entirely by Google. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

FastGallery is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
